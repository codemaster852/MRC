About MRC

MRC is a multi-project brand founded by Mostafa Hesham Alatawy, focusing on technology, education, content creation, and AI integration. The name MRC appears across several digital platforms, each serving a unique purpose in learning, media, competition, and banking.

Our mission is to build an integrated digital ecosystem that supports education through SkillUP and MRC University, creativity and media via MRC Media and MRC News, financial literacy through MRC Bank, motivation and recognition via MRC Awards, and research and development via MRC Resources Bank.
